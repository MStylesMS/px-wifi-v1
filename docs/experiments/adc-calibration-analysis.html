Battery Charge Percentage (SoC) Impact Analysis

The Critical Discovery: Voltage Error ≠ Charge Error

A small voltage error translates to a massive charge percentage error because SLA battery discharge curves are non-linear.

Sealed lead-acid (SLA) batteries do not discharge linearly. The voltage drops slowly at high charge levels, then rapidly during the 80–20% charge window. This means:

  • A 0.2V voltage error at 6.2V could represent a 20% error in charge percentage
  • The device's deep-sleep trigger at 20% SoC (≈5.40V) falls right in this sensitive region
  • Old calibration errors cause the device to think the battery is nearly dead when it still has 40–50% capacity
